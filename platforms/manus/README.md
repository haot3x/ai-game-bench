authorized Manus session. Start a new task, keep observed defaults, submit the

without silently continuing into additional paid attempts. Model identity stays
unknown unless exposed with a source.

## Game handoff

The historical playable artifact was an unpublished, managed port-3000 Preview,
not the task/chat page. Inspect and bind the specific authorized game-only URL;
do not allow arbitrary account pages merely because their domain belongs to
Manus. Record its access requirements, expiry risk and observation time. Preview
availability is not permission to publish/deploy it. The earlier evaluator used
a full game-only viewport, with explicit focus and screenshot/keyboard evidence.
No task URL, preview identifier or account state is copied into this directory.
